# Institutional pricing — rate card

Last updated 2026-08-19. Built from real quotes, not estimates.

## The one rule

**Price DAYS, not draws.** The competitor who beat us at Mass Eye and Ear quoted
**$1,500 for two days** on a 50–100 draw request — they never priced per draw.
That is how research groups, conferences and facilities actually buy this.

Quoting per draw only works with a **minimum attached**, or a light day loses money.

## Quote this

**On-site / event / research day:** **$700–850/day.**
Competitive against the $750 that beat us, and keeps **$200–300/day** over a
$450–550 collector.

**Recurring facility visits** (short stop, a few residents): per-draw with a
visit minimum. Chandler Place is quoted at $55/draw with a $165 minimum —
travel dominates the cost on these, not the draws.

**Always attach a minimum.** Monell's first day had 15 participants show against
a 20-draw minimum; the minimum is the only reason that day was profitable.

## Losses, and why

- **Mass Eye and Ear (2026-08-19)** — quoted **$135/draw**, 7–9× market. Lost
  explicitly on cost. Divya Chari volunteered the winning number: $1,500.
- **Gift of Life (2026-06)** — a provider quoted the buyer $500 directly and
  lost it. Never let a provider quote a B2B buyer cold. We hold the contract.

## Things that are true and cost money to forget

- **Stated volumes are aspirational.** Monell's form said 51–200 draws/month.
  Actual: ~15/day for a handful of days. Never plan revenue off the form.
- **Margin is $230–300 per collector-day.** Five engagement days a month is
  $1,200–1,500. Good second line; not a transformation. Plan against this.
- **Get a provider's day rate at onboarding**, before there's a deal. Three
  deals stalled or died waiting for a cost we could have had on file.
- **Supplies are the collector's**, built into our rate. Monell's add-on is
  $4/draw and covers two tubes.
